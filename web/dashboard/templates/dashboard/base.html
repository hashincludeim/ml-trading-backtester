Up or Down

Next-day stock direction models, backtests and interactive charts.

How it works, with a one-minute video

Data

Prices from Yahoo Finance, adjusted for splits and dividends (stocks in USD, the S&P 500 in index points), refreshed with fetch_prices.

Method

Models are trained offline with chronological splits and time-series cross-validation, then scored on later, unseen days.

Educational project: nothing here is investment advice.
